## Extracted values

- Date and court: 2025-08-19, High Court of Delhi (K&S Partners, Digital Policy Alert).
- Parties: Elsevier, Wiley, and the American Chemical Society sued for copyright
  infringement (LiveLaw).
- Order: the Department of Telecommunications and the Ministry of Electronics and
  Information Technology must have providers block Sci-Hub, with the mirrors
  sci-hub.ru, sci-hub.se, and sci-hub.st, and Sci-Net at sci-net.xyz, within 72
  hours (K&S Partners, LiveLaw).
- The court rejected Elbakyan's argument that Sci-Net was a separate project
  outside her 2020 undertaking (K&S Partners).
- Kind: blocking order, made on an interim application. The suit was still
  pending, listed for 2025-12-01 (LiveLaw, K&S Partners).
