# brein-v-m7-rotterdam-2024: the Dutch order to block Anna's Archive

**Provenance.** Ingested 2026-11-09 by M119 from two sources. The first is
BREIN's Annual Report 2024,
https://stichtingbrein.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Annual-report-2024.pdf. The
agent extracted its text with `pdftotext` to
`cairn/references/sources/brein-annual-report-2024.txt` (gitignored). The second
is TorrentFreak, "Dutch Court Orders ISP to Block 'Anna's Archive' and 'LibGen'",
2024-03-22,
https://torrentfreak.com/dutch-court-orders-isp-to-block-annas-archive-and-libgen-240322/,
read with WebFetch. The session did not find the ruling on rechtspraak.nl.
Pagination: the annual report's page 5.
Extraction: verified 2026-11-09 against the extracted report text and the TorrentFreak report.

**Citation.** Rechtbank Rotterdam (Rotterdam District Court), preliminary relief
ruling of March 2024, Stichting BREIN v. M7/Online.nl. No ECLI or case number
was found.

**Role.** One of Anna's Archive's rulings in `sites.json`, counted for the
Netherlands.

## Extracted values

- Court and kind: "a fourth and fifth dynamic blocking order was issued by the
  Rotterdam District Court in 2024 at the request of BREIN" (report p. 5).
- Parties: "The fourth blocking order against access provider M7/Online.nl
  related to" Library Genesis and Anna's Archive (report p. 5).
- Finding: "The preliminary relief judge found that the platforms Library
  Genesis and Anna's Archive make copyrighted works available to the public on
  a large scale" (report p. 5).
- Order: the provider "must block and keep blocked its customers' access to
  domain names and IP addresses, within 5 business days of being noticed by
  BREIN" (report p. 5).
- Date: TorrentFreak, on 2024-03-22, says that BREIN obtained the order "last
  week", so the sites file gives `2024-03`.
