# elsevier-v-isps-paris-2019: the French order to block Sci-Hub

**Provenance.** Ingested 2026-11-09 by M119 from two news reports, read with
WebFetch:

- Next INpact, 2019-03-30, https://next.ink/6990/107689-les-principaux-fai-francais-doivent-bloquer-sci-hub-et-libgen/

The session did not find the decision's own text. Pagination: none.
Extraction: verified 2026-11-09 against both reports, and the date comes from Next INpact alone.

**Citation.** Tribunal de grande instance de Paris, order of 2019-03-07, on the
request of Elsevier and Springer Nature against Bouygues Télécom, Free, Orange,
and SFR. The reports give no case number.

**Role.** One of Sci-Hub's rulings in `sites.json`, counted for France.

## Extracted values

- Date: 2019-03-07, an interim (référé) order (Next INpact).
- Parties: the publishers Elsevier and Springer Nature asked. The court ordered
  the providers Bouygues Télécom, Free, Orange, and SFR (both reports).
- Order: the four providers must block access to Sci-Hub and LibGen, with 57
  domain names including mirrors, for one year (both reports).
- Basis: the court found that the sites "clearly claim to be pirate platforms
  rejecting the principle of copyright" (TorrentFreak's translation).
- Kind: blocking order. The site was not a party (TorrentFreak).
