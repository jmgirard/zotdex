# elsevier-v-scihub-2017: the United States default judgment that took Sci-Hub's domains

**Provenance.** Ingested 2026-11-09 by M119 from the court's Default Judgment and

https://copyrightalliance.org/copyright-cases/elsevier-inc-v-sci-hub. The agent
fetched it and keeps a copy as `cairn/references/sources/elsevier-v-scihub-2017.pdf`
(gitignored). Pagination: the document's own pages 1 to 4.
Extraction: verified 2026-11-09 against the PDF's text layer (`pdftotext -layout`), which reads cleanly for the quoted lines.

**Citation.** Elsevier Inc. v. Sci-Hub, No. 15-cv-4282 (RWS), United States
District Court for the Southern District of New York, Default Judgment and
Permanent Injunction, Document 87, filed 2017-06-21.

**Role.** One of Sci-Hub's rulings in `sites.json`, counted for the United States.

## Extracted values

- Defendants: "SCI-HUB d/b/a WWW.SCI-HUB.ORG", the Library Genesis Project,
  Alexandra Elbakyan, and John Does 1-99 (p. 1).
- Kind: default judgment. No defendant appeared after the preliminary
  injunction or answered the complaint, and the clerk "entered a default
  against all Defendants on February 16, 2017" (p. 2).
- Finding: the defendants "are liable for willful copyright infringement under
  17 U.S.C. §§ 101, et. seq." (p. 2).
- Order: "The TLD Registries for the Defendants' websites, or their
  administrators shall transfer ownership of the domains listed in Exhibit B
  hereto to Elsevier Inc." (p. 3). Statutory damages of $15,000,000 (p. 3).
- "This is a FINAL JUDGEMENT" (p. 4).
