# M113: Show each plugin's own icon on its page

**Status:** done (2026-11-07, PR #862 https://github.com/jmgirard/zotdex/pull/862)

**Goal:** Each plugin page shows the icon that the plugin's release declares in its manifest, redrawn by the build as a PNG file that Zotdex serves, and shows none where the build cannot read one safely.

**Outcome:** `src/upstream/icon.ts` holds `pickIconPath` (the smallest whole-number key at or above 96, else the largest; one leading `/` or `./` dropped; `..` refused), `iconFormat` (PNG, JPEG, WebP, or SVG by bytes), `redrawIcon` (sharp, imported only there, draws a 96×96 PNG, refuses a side over 4096 px, an SVG with `<image` or `<feImage`, and `TEMPLATE_ICONS`, the zotero-plugin-template stock hashes; draws stop after 10 s), and the reader returns the picked file's bytes as an unpublished `icon`, with any failed read giving none. The build names `upstream.icon` (`icons/<sha>.png`, new optional field in plugins.v1), writes `v1/icons/` before `plugins.json`, best-effort clears other icon files, and drops any icon it does not hold. `iconHidden: { reason }` in `index.json` (new in index.v1, covered by `reason-not-blank`) hides an icon, kept records included. A kept record keeps its icon only when `readPreviousIcons` reads the deployed file back as a 96×96 PNG with the SHA-256 in its name (8 at once, `previousMs`, 64 KiB). The plugin page head is a grid of `.head-text` and `.head-side`, with a white `.icon-tile` (`forced-color-adjust: none`) at the top right. About gains "Icons", and the license line names icons. Docs, README, DESIGN, and CHANGELOG cover it.

**Decisions:** D-080 (icons from the manifest, redrawn and served, shown with a maintainer hide; annotates DESIGN's Contract boundary, D-063, and D-073's use of sharp).

**Review:** Fresh evidence for all 8 criteria, with browser checks at 375 and 1280 px in light and dark. Claim audit: 110 claims, 4 corrected. Four lenses gave 31 findings: 21 fixed (draw time limit and embedded-image refusal, full-width head with no icon, a PNG check on kept icons, required `writeData` icons, best-effort cleanup, lazy sharp, radius tokens, 9 stale doc lines) and 10 rejected (planned or false, including the approved "doesn't look at icons" line). No follow-up rows. CI green, squash-merged. Lessons: 12 marked used M113, one captured (grid gap and sharp SVG links), 2026-11-03 (M089) evicted by rank.
